🌱 Beginner guide — read this and you trade like a professional

Finish this section and the quiz below and you will know more about what actually works than most interview candidates. Let's work this out step by step, from zero to pro:

  1. Step 1 — Prices move in three ways. Up (higher highs and higher lows), down (lower highs and lower lows), or sideways. Everything else is decoration. Our code detects this with fractal swings — no opinions, just geometry.
  2. Step 2 — Big players leave footprints. When buyers accept higher and higher prices into a ceiling (higher lows into resistance), price breaks out 55–58% of the time. That is the single most verified pattern in this study.
  3. Step 3 — Support and resistance are zones, not lines. Prices turn inside areas about half an ATR wide. Drawing a razor-thin line and putting your stop one tick behind it is why beginners get "hunted".
  4. Step 4 — Candles describe, they don't predict. A hammer tells you buyers fought back today. It says almost nothing about tomorrow (verified: ≈ base rate). Trade the reaction at a level, never the candle alone.
  5. Step 5 — Volume marks exhaustion, not confirmation. The heaviest down-days bounce the hardest. Panic is loud; the bounce after panic is the trade, not the panic itself.
  6. Step 6 — Confirmation is the cheapest edge in trading. Waiting one bar for a rejection candle lifted pullback profit factor from 1.41 to 2.28. Patience is a strategy with a measured return.
  7. Step 7 — Costs and out-of-sample kill fantasies. Every backtest here pays commission + slippage and is judged on data it never trained on. Any "strategy" that skips this is a story, not a strategy.

❓ FAQ

So… can I get rich with the 3 confirmed setups?Probably not directly — edges are small after costs (profit factors 1.2–2.3) and this is daily-bar, educational research, not financial advice. The value is knowing what not to pay for.
Why do down-days bounce?Mean-reversion on daily equities: panic exhausts sellers and the bounce follows: our DU cell (down + high volume) leads 5-day drift on all four symbols (+0.37% to +0.95%).
What is a hikkake / trap tax?About 5% of bars are failed inside-bar breakouts that trap breakout traders. Any breakout system pays this base rate before costs — size positions accordingly.
Can I add my own strategy?Yes: write a detector returning a boolean Series (signal at close t), pass it to backtest_signals, and the harness handles costs, OOS split and stats. See experiments/run_all.py E07 for the template.
